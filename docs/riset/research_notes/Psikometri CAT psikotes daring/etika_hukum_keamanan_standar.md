# Legal, ethical and technical-standard requirements for an Indonesian online psychological selection test (psikotes daring / CAT)

Checked 2026-10-08. These are research notes, not legal advice. "Primary" means I read the official text (salinan or the standard's own PDF). Anything else is marked as secondary or unverified.

## 1. Indonesian psychology law: UU No. 23 Tahun 2022 tentang Pendidikan dan Layanan Psikologi

### Takeaway
UU 23/2022 reserves "Layanan Psikologi" for a **Psikolog** (a graduate of the pendidikan profesi who holds STR + SILP). It also names "pengukuran psikologis" as a jasa Psikologi that must use "alat ukur psikologis yang sahih dan andal". An operational selection psikotes that is interpreted for a client is therefore a Psikolog's service. The non-Psikolog team may only assist ("dibantu oleh atau bekerja sama dengan lulusan pendidikan akademik Psikologi"). The implementing PP was still not enacted as of the last evidence I found (harmonisasi RPP, March 2026), so details on mechanisms, kewenangan and sanctions are still pending.

### Cited Findings (primary text: salinan UU 23/2022 hosted by IPK Indonesia)
Source for all of the following: [UU 23/2022 salinan (ipkindonesia.or.id)](https://www.ipkindonesia.or.id/media/2022/08/Undang_Undang_No_23_Tahun_2022_tentang_Pendidikan_dan_Layanan_Psikologi.pdf). Mirror: [peraturan.go.id](https://peraturan.go.id/id/uu-no-23-tahun-2022).

- **Pasal 1 angka 3**, "Layanan Psikologi adalah segala aktivitas pemberian jasa dan praktik Psikologi yang memerlukan kompetensi sebagai Psikolog …".
- **Pasal 1 angka 7–8**, STR = Surat Tanda Registrasi. **SILP** = "Surat Izin Layanan Psikologi … bukti tertulis pemberian kewenangan kepada Psikolog untuk memberikan Layanan Psikologi". The law's term is **SILP**, not "SIPP". SIPP is HIMPSI's older/organisational term, see for example [SK HIMPSI 049/SK/PP-HIMPSI/IX/24 tentang SKP-SIPP](https://himpsijawabarat.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/049-SK-SKP-SIPP-2024.pdf) (title only, not read).
- **Pasal 1 angka 4**, "Standar Layanan" = criteria and benchmark for professional Layanan Psikologi.
- **Pasal 3 ayat (2)**, Layanan Psikologi asas: nilai ilmiah, etika, profesionalitas, **nondiskriminasi**, manfaat, kepedulian, **kerahasiaan**, pemberdayaan.
- **Pasal 15**, STR is issued by "induk organisasi profesi himpunan Psikologi" (in practice HIMPSI) and "berlaku seumur hidup".
- **Pasal 17**, "Setiap Psikolog yang menjalankan Layanan Psikologi wajib memiliki SILP". SILP is issued by Pemerintah Pusat and valid 2 years at first issue (program profesi) or 5 years (spesialis). **Pasal 18**: extension needs a valid STR + HIMPSI recommendation; extended SILP lasts 5 years.
- **Pasal 25**, a Psikolog practising outside the jenis layanan on their STR/SILP, or without SILP, faces sanksi administratif: denda, pencabutan SILP and/or STR. The procedure is left to a PP.
- **Pasal 26 ayat (1)–(2)**, "Layanan Psikologi dilaksanakan oleh Psikolog sesuai dengan kewenangannya". A Psikolog "dapat dibantu oleh atau bekerja sama dengan lulusan pendidikan akademik Psikologi". **Ayat (4)**: Layanan can be "secara langsung dan/atau tidak langsung", which is the hook for online or indirect delivery. **Ayat (5)**: perorangan or kelembagaan.
- **Pasal 27**, consent: given orally or in writing (ayat 1), "setelah mendapat informasi mengenai manfaat dan/atau risiko terkait pemeriksaan" (ayat 2). If the Klien cannot consent, a parent/guardian or another party gives it (ayat 4). Consent is not required in disaster emergencies, for humanitarian reasons or for law enforcement (ayat 5).
- **Pasal 32–33**, jasa Psikologi includes "**pengukuran psikologis**" (Pasal 33 ayat 1 huruf a). Ayat (2) says: "Pengukuran psikologis … merupakan pemeriksaan psikologis dengan menggunakan **alat ukur psikologis yang sahih dan andal**." Ayat (6): "Psikolog dapat mengembangkan alat ukur Psikologi."
- **Pasal 35**, new service forms can be added "sesuai dengan perkembangan ilmu pengetahuan dan teknologi" by Pemerintah Pusat after coordinating with HIMPSI.
- **Pasal 36**, the mechanism for providing Layanan Psikologi (Pasal 26–35) is "diatur dengan Peraturan Pemerintah".
- **Pasal 37 ayat (2)**, Standar Layanan must at least contain: (a) sarana dan prasarana; (b) **standar pemeriksaan psikologis**; (c) "**standar penyimpanan laporan hasil layanan dalam bentuk cetak dan/atau digital**"; (d) standar etika Psikologi Indonesia. **Ayat (3)**: drafted by HIMPSI and set by Pemerintah Pusat.
- **Pasal 42**, kewajiban Psikolog: (c) explain the institution's function, the Psikolog's role and the "prosedur pemeriksaan sejak awal hingga akhir" orally and/or in writing; (d) examine in line with consent; (f) "menjaga kerahasiaan dan menghormati hak Klien". **Pasal 43**: sanctions are teguran lisan, peringatan tertulis, denda administratif, pencabutan SILP.
- **Pasal 44**, hak Klien: (a) an explanation of the procedure; (b) a service in line with Standar Layanan; (c) consent/agreement; (d) "jaminan kerahasiaan kondisi dan data pribadi Klien"; (e) "**menyampaikan keluhan … dan memperoleh tanggapan**" (complaints/appeals); (f) "**mendapatkan penjelasan yang benar dan jelas terkait hasil Layanan Psikologi**" (feedback right).
- **Pasal 51 huruf b–c (peralihan)**, existing practice permits stay valid for at most 5 years "sejak peraturan pelaksanaan Undang-Undang ini diundangkan".
- **Pasal 56**, implementing regulations "harus ditetapkan paling lama 2 (dua) tahun" after promulgation. The UU was promulgated in August 2022, so the deadline was about August 2024.
- Status of the implementing PP: a "rapat lanjutan harmonisasi RPP Pendidikan dan Layanan Psikologi" was held on 13 March 2026, after a first harmonisation meeting on 10 March 2026 ([DJPP Kemenkum](https://djpp.kemenkum.go.id/publikasi/indeks-berita/rapat-lanjutan-harmonisasi-rpp-pendidikan-dan-layanan-psikologi-perkuat-implementasi-uu-psikologi)). I found no evidence the PP was enacted by 2026-10-08.

### Inferences
- An online platform that only *delivers* items, with no interpretation, is arguably a tool. Once it produces interpretive reports used for a selection decision, it is "pengukuran psikologis" and needs a named Psikolog holding STR and SILP, acting "secara kelembagaan" (for example a Biro Psikologi) or individually. The platform should record which Psikolog is responsible for each test session or report.
- UU 23/2022 has no category called "Ilmuwan Psikologi". That term comes from the HIMPSI Kode Etik. Under the UU, S1/S2 non-profesi graduates may only assist a Psikolog (Pasal 26(2)).
- Pasal 33(2) "sahih dan andal" is the legal hook for publishing validity and reliability evidence, i.e. a technical manual.
- Pasal 37(2)(c) means a digital retention/storage standard for result reports will come from HIMPSI and the Government. It is not yet published, so for now design to the PDP rules.

### Gaps
- No enacted PP for UU 23/2022 was found, and I did not find the RPP text. The final rules on online ("tidak langsung") services, kewenangan per level, sanction procedures and the Standar Layanan contents are unknown.
- The deadline for SILP issuance in practice, and which ministry issues SILP, could not be verified.

## 2. HIMPSI Kode Etik Psikologi Indonesia (2010) and HIMPSI guidance on online testing

### Takeaway
The 2010 Kode Etik (Kongres XI HIMPSI) has a chapter on Asesmen, **Bab XI, Pasal 62–67**. It covers: the basis of assessment, use of assessment, informed consent (with an explicit exception for routine selection and exams), interpretation that accounts for culture and language, release of data and results (raw data belongs to the examiner, not to the client company), and the duty to keep test tools, data and results secure. I could not obtain the official HIMPSI PDF, so the text below comes from secondary and academic reproductions. I found no primary HIMPSI document on online testing.

### Cited Findings
- Structure: 14 bab, 80 pasal; Bab XI "Asesmen" = Pasal 62–67; produced by Kongres XI HIMPSI 2010 ([RPS/handout listings via search, e.g. UPJ handout](https://ocw.upj.ac.id/files/Handout-PSY204-Kode-Etik-Psikologi-Indonesia.pdf); [Binadarma RPS Kode Etik 2024](https://psikologi.binadarma.ac.id/document/1709450652_RPS%20KODE%20ETIK%202024.pdf)). Secondary; I could not fetch the official PDF (HTTP 403).
- **Pasal 64 (Informed consent dalam asesmen)**, consent is required except when (a) the assessment is mandated by government regulation or law; (b) consent is implied because the assessment "dilakukan sebagai bagian dari kegiatan pendidikan, kelembagaan atau organisasi secara rutin misal: **seleksi, ujian**"; (c) the assessment evaluates an individual's capacity for decision-making ([Studocu reproduction of Pasal 65–66](https://www.studocu.id/id/document/universitas-islam-negeri-sunan-kalijaga-yogyakarta/psikologi/pasal-6566/46883631); [pdfcoffee Bab XI](https://pdfcoffee.com/download/bab-xi-kode-etik-psikologi-indonesia-pdf-free.html)). Secondary.
- **Pasal 65 (Interpretasi hasil asesmen)**, the Psikolog must consider instrument factors and test-taker characteristics, "keadaan situasional …, bahasa dan perbedaan budaya", which can affect interpretation accuracy (same secondary sources).
- **Pasal 66 (Penyampaian data dan hasil asesmen)**, "Data asesmen Psikologi adalah data alat/instrumen psikologi yang berupa data kasar, respon terhadap pertanyaan atau stimulus, catatan serta rekam psikologis. Data asesmen ini menjadi kewenangan Psikolog dan/atau Ilmuwan Psikologi yang melakukan pemeriksaan." Raw data may be passed only to fellow professionals for follow-up (same secondary sources).
- **Pasal 67 (Menjaga alat, data dan hasil asesmen)**, "wajib menjaga kelengkapan dan keamanan instrumen/alat tes psikologi, data asesmen psikologi dan hasil asesmen psikologi sesuai dengan kewenangan …" (same secondary sources).
- Confidentiality (in a reproduction of the Kode Etik): Pasal 24 requires keeping client secrets and limiting disclosure to authorised parties directly involved, and Pasal 26(5) lists exceptions for legal purposes ([psikologipolresmalangkota.wordpress.com](https://psikologipolresmalangkota.wordpress.com/2013/01/04/kode-etik-psikologi/)). The same page defines **Psikolog** as a graduate of professional psychology education (S1 + profesi or S2 Psikolog) holding a practice licence, and **Ilmuwan Psikologi** as an expert with an S1/S2/S3 psychology background.
- **Conflict:** the same wordpress reproduction numbers the Asesmen articles as Pasal 57–62 and includes a "Pasal 58(3)" that classifies tests by APA **Level A / B / C** (A: non-clinical, "dapat dilakukan oleh siapa saja"; B: needs psychology knowledge such as statistics and individual differences; C: needs understanding of testing plus supervised experience). This numbering does not match the widely cited 2010 numbering (Pasal 62–67). It may be an older or draft version. **Treat the A/B/C clause as unverified for the 2010 text.**
- Secondary reports say HIMPSI issued a "tabel klasifikasi alat tes psikologi" and minimum criteria for a test session (stated purpose, at least 120 minutes, at least 1 method, at least 2 tools) ([search summary of UNUSIA conference paper "Tes Psikologi Online pada Masa Pandemi"](https://journal.unusia.ac.id/index.php/Conferenceunusia/article/download/198/148/547)). Unverified; I did not read the primary HIMPSI document.
- HIMPSI and AP2TPI formed a team on telepsychology guidelines. A Delphi study identified 93 competencies for telekonseling, including "pelaksanaan asesmen dan evaluasi" ([Gadjah Mada J. of Psychology, Identifikasi Kompetensi Psikolog dalam Telekonseling](https://journal.ugm.ac.id/gamajop/article/view/71141)). The online PKPP exam evaluation "must refer to quality standards issued by AP2TPI and HIMPSI" ([FMIPA Unpad SOP](https://fmipa.unpad.ac.id/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/025-SOP-Evaluasi-Ujian-PKPP-Daring-FMIPA.pdf)).
- Academic commentary: "kursus tes psikologis" (psikotes coaching that leaks real items) is argued to breach the Kode Etik, and real items circulating online undermines assessment integrity ([Jurnal Filsafat Indonesia, Undiksha, "Dimensi Etis Pelaksanaan Kursus Tes Psikologis"](https://ejournal.undiksha.ac.id/index.php/JFI/article/download/31318/18184/76164)). Search snippet only; the fetch returned 403.

### Inferences
- For selection use, Pasal 64(b) means formal individual consent may not be ethically required under the Kode Etik. It is still required under **UU 23/2022 Pasal 27** (consent after being informed of benefits and risks) and under **PDP** (explicit consent or another lawful basis). Build an explicit consent screen anyway.
- Pasal 66 shapes the data model: raw responses and item-level data should be visible only to the responsible Psikolog. Client HR users should get only the result report. Raw data should not be exported to non-professionals.
- Pasal 67 plus the anti-"kursus" stance: a "latihan/simulasi" product must **not** reuse the operational item bank. Practice items should be separate, publicly shareable items. This matches ITC 3c (see section 4).

### Gaps
- I could not get the official HIMPSI 2010 Kode Etik PDF from himpsi.or.id. Article text is from secondary reproductions.
- I found no evidence of a formally ratified post-2010 revision of the Kode Etik as of 2026-10-08. I did not search HIMPSI's site deeply, so this is unconfirmed.
- I found no primary HIMPSI pedoman or surat edaran on online or remote psychological testing (COVID era or later). The search surfaced only secondary mentions.
- The HIMPSI test classification table (which tests are restricted to Psikolog) could not be retrieved.

## 3. Personal data: UU 27/2022 (PDP) and PP 33/2026 (implementing regulation)

### Takeaway
UU PDP has been fully in force since 17 Oct 2024. Its implementing regulation, **PP No. 33 Tahun 2026** (225 pasal, ditetapkan and diundangkan 16 Juli 2026), takes effect **16 Januari 2027** (6 months after promulgation). For a psikotes platform, the key points are:
- Psych-test results are at least personal data, and plausibly "data dan Informasi kesehatan" (data spesifik), especially for any clinical or mental-health content.
- **Scoring/evaluation processing triggers a mandatory DPIA** (PP 33 Pasal 120(2)(d)).
- Breach notification within **3×24 jam** to data subjects and the Lembaga.
- A written retention policy is required.
- Hosting in Supabase Singapore is a cross-border transfer, which requires a transfer assessment, prior notice to subjects, and an adequacy finding, binding safeguards (standard contract clauses) or consent.

### Cited Findings
- UU 27/2022 Pasal 4(2), data spesifik: "data dan informasi kesehatan; data biometrik; data genetika; catatan kejahatan; data anak; data keuangan pribadi" ([pasal.id UU 27/2022](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-27-tahun-2022); official: [peraturan.go.id](https://peraturan.go.id/id/uu-no-27-tahun-2022)).
- UU PDP Pasal 10(1), right to object to "pengambilan keputusan yang hanya didasarkan pada pemrosesan secara otomatis". Pasal 9: withdraw consent. Pasal 20(2): six lawful bases. Pasal 25: children's data needs parent/guardian consent. Pasal 26: data of penyandang disabilitas. Pasal 34: DPIA. Pasal 35–39: security and confidentiality. Pasal 44: deletion/destruction. **Pasal 46(1): written breach notice within 3×24 jam**. Pasal 51–52: processors. Pasal 53: DPO. **Pasal 56: transfer abroad** (equivalent protection → binding safeguards → consent). Pasal 57: administrative fine up to 2% of annual revenue. Pasal 67: criminal penalties. Pasal 74: 2-year transition to 17 Oct 2024 ([pasal.id](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-27-tahun-2022)). This is a secondary aggregator summary; the article numbers match my knowledge of the primary text.
- **PP 33/2026, primary salinan** ([PP 33/2026 salinan, hosted by Pemkab Banyumas](https://static.banyumaskab.go.id/website/file/peraturan_pemerintah_no._33_tahun_2026_100926115902.pdf)):
  - **Pasal 225**: "mulai berlaku setelah 6 (enam) bulan terhitung sejak tanggal diundangkan". Ditetapkan and diundangkan in Jakarta on **16 Juli 2026** (signed by Prabowo Subianto). The in-force date is therefore 16 Jan 2027, confirmed by [Hukumonline Pro](https://pro.hukumonline.com/a/lt6a97c3fe4527c/pemerintah-akhirnya-terbitkan-kerangka-implementasi-pelindungan-data-pribadi--berlaku-mulai-16-januari-2027).
  - **Pasal 6(1)**: data spesifik = data dan Informasi kesehatan, biometrik, genetika, catatan kejahatan, **data Anak**, keuangan pribadi, others set by a Kementerian/lembaga.
  - **Pasal 7(1)**: data umum includes nama, jenis kelamin, kewarganegaraan, agama, status perkawinan, and combinations that identify a person.
  - **Pasal 30(3)**: dasar pemrosesan; huruf a is "persetujuan yang sah secara eksplisit … untuk 1 (satu) atau beberapa tujuan tertentu". **Pasal 31**: processing must not be discriminatory. **Pasal 32(2)**: consent must be "bebas, sadar, spesifik, dan tidak ambigu". **Pasal 33**: information must be given *before* consent, "secara ringkas, tepat". **Pasal 34**: a consent mechanism (electronic allowed) linked to that information. **Pasal 35(3)**: no deceptive or misleading consent. **Pasal 36**: the controller must be able to show *bukti persetujuan* (proof of consent). **Pasal 37**: consent can be withdrawn at any time.
  - **Pasal 75**: the controller must set a **dokumen kebijakan Masa Retensi**. Minimum contents: the period, the data subjects and data types covered, de-identification rules for statistics and research, electronic and non-electronic destruction methods, rules for processors, the person responsible, and the legal basis.
  - Access-right article (around Pasal 76–77): the controller must refuse a subject's access/change request if it would "membahayakan keamanan, kesehatan fisik, atau **kesehatan mental** Subjek Data Pribadi dan/atau orang lain".
  - **Pasal 114**: on a Kegagalan Pelindungan Data Pribadi, written notice "paling lambat 3 x 24 jam" to the Subjek Data Pribadi and the Lembaga, counted from when the failure is "diketahui secara pasti, patut, dan wajar". The notice must state the data exposed, when and how, remediation steps, and the PPDP/contact person.
  - **Pasal 120(1)–(2)**: a DPIA is mandatory for high-risk processing, which includes: (a) "pengambilan keputusan secara otomatis yang memiliki akibat hukum atau dampak yang signifikan"; (b) "pemrosesan atas Data Pribadi yang bersifat spesifik"; (c) large scale; (d) "**pemrosesan Data Pribadi untuk kegiatan evaluasi, penskoran, atau pemantauan yang sistematis**"; (f) "penggunaan teknologi baru". **Pasal 121**: the DPIA is done *before* processing. Minimum contents: description and purpose, necessity and proportionality, risk to rights, and mitigations. It must be documented and reviewed when risk changes.
  - **Pasal 123**: the controller must secure the data through technical and operational measures (text continues beyond my excerpt).
  - **Pasal 142**: a **PPDP** (pejabat/petugas pelindungan data pribadi, i.e. the DPO) is mandatory for public services, for regular systematic large-scale monitoring, or for large-scale processing of data spesifik.
  - **Pasal 160–165 (Transfer ke luar wilayah hukum)**: the controller must map the transfer lifecycle, minimise data, identify and assess the legal instrument, and use contractual/technical/organisational supplementary measures, re-evaluating periodically (Pasal 161–163). **Pasal 164**: inform the subject *before* the transfer of its purpose, the safeguards, how rights are preserved, and the risks and mitigations. **Pasal 165**: (1) the destination country must have equal or higher protection; (2) failing that, "Pelindungan Data Pribadi yang memadai dan bersifat mengikat"; (3) failing both, "wajib mendapatkan persetujuan Subjek Data Pribadi". **Pasal 168**: the Lembaga sets a list of adequate countries. **Pasal 169–170**: binding safeguards include **standar klausul kontrak** set by the Lembaga and binding corporate rules.
  - **Pasal 223 (peralihan)**: until Peraturan Lembaga are issued, processing continues "sepanjang tidak bertentangan" with the PP.
- Status of the supervisory agency: Kompas headlined that the PP was issued while the "lembaga pengawas masih dinanti" ([Kompas.id](https://www.kompas.id/artikel/peraturan-pelaksana-uu-pdp-akhirnya-terbit-lembaga-pengawas-masih-dinanti); paywalled, headline only). Many PP 33 details are delegated to "Peraturan Lembaga" (combination rules, DPIA detail, PPDP competence, standard contract clauses, adequacy list).
- Other electronic-system rules: under PP 71/2019 (PSTE), a PSE Lingkup Privat "dapat melakukan pengelolaan, pemrosesan, dan/atau penyimpanan … di wilayah Indonesia dan/atau di luar wilayah Indonesia". Every PSE Privat must register with Kominfo/Komdigi (Permenkominfo 5/2020); failing to register risks access blocking (Pasal 7) ([Hukumonline on PP PSTE](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/pp-pste-dikhawatirkan-ganggu-kedaulatan-data-lt5dc0b5f2420a8); [Hukumonline Klinik on PSE registration](https://www.hukumonline.com/klinik/a/kewajiban-penyelenggara-sistem-elektronik-melakukan-pendaftaran-lt598832e6a5643/)). Secondary.
- Supabase regions: Southeast Asia is served by Singapore (ap-southeast-1). The official docs list **no Jakarta/Indonesia region** ([Supabase docs: Available regions](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/regions)). Per search summary; the page itself was not fetched.

### Inferences
- **Are psikotes results data spesifik?** Neither UU PDP nor PP 33 (in what I read) names "hasil tes psikologi" explicitly. Personality or clinical-type scales (e.g. neuroticism, MMPI-type content, mental-health screening) are arguably "data dan Informasi kesehatan", so treat them as data spesifik. Pure ability/aptitude scores are less clear. Safest design: classify all psikotes results as data spesifik. Whatever the classification, **Pasal 120(2)(d) "evaluasi, penskoran" already makes a DPIA mandatory** for any psychometric scoring.
- Selection decisions that rely only on automated cut-offs trigger UU PDP Pasal 10 (right to object) and PP 33 Pasal 120(2)(a). Keep a human Psikolog in the loop and document it.
- Supabase Singapore: Singapore has the PDPA and a regulator (PDPC), so it may qualify under Pasal 168. The Lembaga's adequacy list does not exist yet, so rely on Pasal 165(2)–(3) for now: a DPA with Supabase or the Lembaga's standard clauses once issued, **plus** explicit consent and the Pasal 164 pre-transfer notice. Keep a written transfer assessment (Pasal 161–163).
- Minimum compliance artefacts before 16 Jan 2027: privacy notice + consent log with proof (Pasal 33–36); DPIA (Pasal 121); retention policy (Pasal 75); breach runbook within 3×24 h (Pasal 114); records of processing; processor agreement with Supabase/Vercel; transfer assessment; PPDP if the scale is large; PSE registration.

### Gaps
- The UU PDP Penjelasan for Pasal 4(2)(a), i.e. whether "kesehatan mental" is explicitly part of "data dan informasi kesehatan", was not verified from the primary Penjelasan text. The BPK download link returned an unrelated document.
- The Lembaga PDP's establishment status, adequacy list and standard contract clauses as of Oct 2026 are not confirmed.
- No Indonesian rule specific to retention periods for psychological test data was found. UU 23/2022 Pasal 37(2)(c) defers this to a Standar Layanan that does not exist yet.

## 4. International standards for computer-based and internet testing

### Takeaway
Use these as the professional benchmark set:
- **ITC Guidelines on Computer-Based and Internet Delivered Testing (2005)**: four administration modes, item exposure, authentication and cheating control, security and privacy.
- **ITC Guidelines on the Security of Tests, Examinations, and Other Assessments (2014)**: security plan, breach response, data forensics.
- **ITC/ATP Guidelines for Technology-Based Assessment (Nov 2022)**: the most current document, 11 chapters including disruptions/resume, automated and AI scoring, privacy, and WCAG.
- **ISO 10667-2:2020**: requirements for assessment service providers in work settings.
- **ISO/IEC 27001**: ISMS certification recommended by ITC/ATP.

### Cited Findings
**ITC CBT/Internet Guidelines (v1.0, 1 July 2005; doc ITC-G-CB-20140617), primary** ([ITC PDF](https://www.intestcom.org/files/guideline_computer_based_testing.pdf)):
- Four modes. **Open**: no supervision, identity cannot be authenticated. **Controlled**: no supervision, test available only to known test-takers with a logon. **Supervised (Proctored)**: an administrator logs in the candidate and confirms correct administration. **Managed**: high control, as in a dedicated test centre.
- Publishers should "specify and restrict" tests per scenario. "Psychometric tests for use in post-sift selection testing and/or post-hire assessment normally would not be available in open mode" (guideline 38.2).
- **Lost connections (1b)**: "minimise the impact of hang-ups, lost Internet connections … the system should ensure that no information is lost when the Internet connection is lost" (4.5). Publishers must "put procedures in place to deal fairly with the impact of hang-ups, lost connections" (5.6). Timed tests: on-screen response within 2 seconds, and transition/recording time must not count against the timer (4.3–4.4).
- **Equivalence (2c)**: if a computerised version derives from paper, document comparable reliabilities, cross-version correlations, external correlations, and comparable means/SDs. Equivalence and norming studies for internet tests should be run "over the Internet … under conditions that represent those that the intended target population will experience (e.g., unproctored)" (22.3). Do not use a test in an unproctored mode if it is specified for proctored use only (24.4).
- **Scoring (2d)**: document the accuracy and validity of scoring rules. When reports classify people (e.g. "High sales potential"), document classification accuracy and the rationale behind computer-based test interpretation (CBTI) statements (25.1–25.4).
- **Feedback (2e)**: state the limits of CBTI. Unproctored administration and unverifiable identity must be flagged in interpretation. Internet feedback must handle negative-feedback ethics and point to support (30.9).
- **Equality of access (2f)**: document fairness methods and assess **DIF** (31.1–31.2).
- **Item exposure (3c)**: for high stakes, equalise item exposure rates from item banks, keep banks large enough for parallel forms or CAT, generate unique forms, and restrict fixed forms to supervised/managed modes where cheating is prevalent. Practice must be provided "without compromising the security of the actual test items" (40–42).
- **Authenticity and cheating (3d)**: for moderate/high-stakes selection in controlled mode, "those obtaining qualifying scores should be required to take a supervised test to confirm their scores". Use a consistency check between original and confirmation responses, an explicit honesty policy accepted in advance, and a list of consequences for fraud (44–45). Supervised/managed modes require government photo ID (45.1).
- **Security of materials (4a)**: block copy/print/download. "All important intellectual property (e.g., scoring rules, norms, interpretation algorithms) … should remain on the host server" (47.1). Passwords only for qualified users. Monitor the web for leaked items, and keep a breach adjudication process with sanctions (47.9).
- **Data in transit and at rest (4b–4c)**: encryption, confirmation that the server received the data, frequent backups with a disaster-recovery plan, the ability to access/check/delete data under local law, tamper detection ("detect unauthorised changes"), and disclosure of who can access data, for what purpose and for how long (49–53).

**ITC Security Guidelines (v1.0, 6 July 2014; ITC-G-TS-20140706), primary** ([ITC PDF](https://www.intestcom.org/files/guideline_test_security.pdf)):
- Three parts: (1) developing and implementing a **security plan** (roles, test-taker rights and responsibilities, a breach action plan, a privacy-law reference, periodic audit of providers); (2) security across the process; (3) responding to breaches.
- **Re-testing policies** limit item harvesting, e.g. no retake after passing, or a minimum waiting period. Monitor registrations to stop over-testing.
- Designs that limit exposure: CAT, LOFT, MST, multiple equivalent forms, early stopping. Consider forward-only navigation. After a break, earlier items should not be reviewable.
- Test takers must agree to the rules before launch, and those who refuse do not test. Test-taker rights must be documented.
- Authentication "if permitted by prevailing laws": photo ID, biometrics, keystroke dynamics, facial recognition. "Authentication is not the same as identification": you only need to verify that the test taker is the same person who registered.
- Use a lockdown/secure browser. Remote proctoring by webcam is allowed "if permitted by prevailing laws". Proctors must have no stake in the outcome and must file irregularity reports.
- Results should be transferred to the server "immediately after the completion of a test or after the completion of each item", with strong encryption.
- **Data forensics** indicators: abnormal response patterns, **abnormally short or long response times**, response similarity between pairs, similar latency patterns, unusual score gains, item parameter drift (replace compromised items), and performance on Trojan-horse or pretest items versus operational items.

**ITC/ATP Guidelines for Technology-Based Assessment (8 Nov 2022), primary** ([ITC PDF](https://www.intestcom.org/upload/media-library/guidelines-for-technology-based-assessment-v20221108-16684036687NAG8.pdf); [SIOP summary](https://www.siop.org/tip-article/new-guidelines-for-technology-based-assessment/)):
- 11 chapters: development, design/assembly, delivery environments, scoring, reporting, data management, psychometric/technical quality, security, privacy, fairness/accessibility, global/translation.
- **3.6**: "Scoring should be conducted at the server level to prevent compromise or subversion at the browser level."
- **3.9**: the platform should let test takers "resume the test (where they stopped or as close to it as possible) after a service disruption or a planned break".
- **3.11**: capture error messages and details "to diagnose administration errors and facilitate data recovery across multiple sessions".
- **3.13**: ethics of negative feedback, especially for personality and job-fit assessments.
- **3.14–3.15**: state the required testing conditions and the authentication level in advance, scaled to the stakes.
- **3.16**: monitoring by in-person or camera-based remote proctoring "if the stakes … warrant". Test takers must be told the monitoring type in advance, including legally required information.
- **3.24**: locked-down browsers for remote proctoring should detect virtual cameras, virtual microphones and duplicate input devices.
- **4.21–4.24**: log all disruptions per test taker, recover data, and only impute missing scores with empirically validated methods.
- **4.26**: response times should be recorded precisely, avoiding construct-irrelevant technology effects.
- **4.3–4.15**: QC and validity/fairness evaluation of automated and AI scoring, with a representative training sample.
- **7.4**: score comparability across devices and conditions must be evidenced (measurement invariance, DIF).
- **Ch. 9 Privacy**: comply with local law (9.1), follow cross-border transfer rules (9.2), take care with biometrics (9.3), written processor agreements (9.5), data minimisation (9.6), a record of processing reviewed at least annually (9.7), defined retention per data type and deletion after it (9.9–9.10), a privacy notice and advance notice of rights (9.11–9.12), breach notification (9.13), disclosure of automated decision-making and AI, with appeals adapted for AI (9.14–9.16), and an annual third-party security evaluation (9.20).
- **ISO 27001**: "Where possible, certification against ISO 27001:2013 is desirable" (data-management chapter). It also cites CSA STAR and SOC 2 for cloud vendors.

**ISO**:
- **ISO 10667-2:2020**, *Assessment service delivery: Procedures and methods to assess people in work and organizational settings, Part 2: Requirements for service providers* (published Nov 2020). It sets requirements for service providers working with a client to assess individuals or groups for work purposes ([ISO 74717](https://www.iso.org/standard/74717.html)). Part 1 covers the client side (not fetched).

**Test-user qualification benchmarks**:
- **BPS**: three levels, Assistant Test User / Test User / Specialist in Test Use, across educational, forensic and occupational domains. An Assistant Test User can administer and score under supervision but cannot choose tests or interpret beyond standard reports. The historic "Level A" covered ability and "Level B" personality ([BPS qualifications](https://www.bps.org.uk/qualifications-and-fees)). **EFPA**: three competence levels across health, work and education, and BPS qualifications align with them ([EFPA BoA documents 2023](https://www.efpa.eu/sites/default/files/2023-08/Important_BoA_documents_2023.pdf)). Per search summaries.
- **Pearson (publisher practice derived from APA tiers)**: every product carries qualification level A, B or C, "with C being the highest". Purchase rights are hierarchical, and qualification is based on degree, licence or membership ([Pearson Assessments qualifications](https://www.pearsonassessments.com/professional-assessments/ordering/how-to-order/qualifications.html)).

### Inferences
- Map the platform's modes explicitly:
  - "Latihan/simulasi" = **open** mode with public items.
  - Unproctored screening = **controlled** mode, with a login and honesty agreement.
  - Final selection = **supervised** mode (remote webcam or onsite proctor), or confirmatory re-testing of passers per ITC 45.3.
- Supabase implications:
  - Keys, norms and interpretation rules stay server-side (ITC 47.1; ITC/ATP 3.6). Never ship answer keys to the client bundle, and score in Postgres functions or Edge Functions.
  - Save each answer to the server immediately (ITC security 19), which also enables resume (ITC/ATP 3.9).
  - Keep the event log append-only and tamper-evident (ITC 52.2).
  - Store the item-level response time per item for forensics (ITC security 20b; ITC/ATP 4.26).
- Scoring reproducibility (inferred from ITC 25 and 52): version the item bank, keys and norm tables, and record the version IDs used in every scored session so any score can be recomputed.

### Gaps
- **ISO/IEC 23988:2007** (code of practice for IT in assessment delivery): I could not verify its current status (withdrawn, confirmed or revised) from ISO's catalogue.
- **ISO 10667-1** contents, the **EFPA Test Review Model** criteria and the **AERA/APA/NCME Standards (2014)** were not fetched. Their specific chapter/standard numbers are not cited here.
- **ITC Guidelines for Translating and Adapting Tests (2nd ed., 2017)** were not fetched. They are relevant if foreign tests are translated into Bahasa Indonesia.
- No Indonesian (SNI) adoption of ISO 10667 was found (not searched in depth).

## 5. Test security and integrity in practice (proctoring, lockdown, exposure, identity, response-time analytics, session resume, audit)

### Takeaway
The standards scale controls to the stakes, and none of them requires AI proctoring. The professional minimum for online selection is:
- a known-user login
- an accepted honesty agreement
- server-side keys and scoring
- per-item autosave and resume
- randomised or parallel forms from an item bank
- a retake policy
- logs of response time and disruptions
- forensics review
- for decision-grade results, a supervised or confirmatory session

Any camera, biometric or AI monitoring must be disclosed in advance and is high-risk processing under PP 33.

### Cited Findings
- Authentication levels and confirmatory supervised testing for selection candidates who passed an unproctored stage: ITC CBT 44–45 ([ITC 2005](https://www.intestcom.org/files/guideline_computer_based_testing.pdf)).
- Lockdown browser, remote webcam proctoring "if permitted by prevailing laws", proctor independence, irregularity reports, forensics indicators including response times and latency similarity: ITC security guideline 17–20 ([ITC 2014](https://www.intestcom.org/files/guideline_test_security.pdf)).
- Remote proctoring lockdown should detect virtual cameras, virtual microphones and duplicate input devices (3.24). Monitoring type must be disclosed in advance (3.16). Resume after disruption (3.9). Log all disruptions (4.21) ([ITC/ATP 2022](https://www.intestcom.org/upload/media-library/guidelines-for-technology-based-assessment-v20221108-16684036687NAG8.pdf)).
- Biometric data (including face recognition for ID checks) is **data spesifik** under PP 33 Pasal 6(1)(b). A "penggunaan teknologi baru" or "pemantauan yang sistematis" triggers a DPIA (Pasal 120(2)(d),(f)) ([PP 33/2026](https://static.banyumaskab.go.id/website/file/peraturan_pemerintah_no._33_tahun_2026_100926115902.pdf)).
- Exposure control: item banks large enough for parallel forms or CAT, exposure-rate balancing, no piloting on live tests, unique forms per candidate (ITC CBT 40). Retest waiting periods (ITC security Part 2 item 4).

### Inferences
- A browser-only web app cannot truly lock down a device. Visibility/blur events, copy-paste blocking and fullscreen requests are deterrents and signals, not controls. Document this limit (ITC 4a asks for "features that minimise the risk", not guarantees). Treat tab-switch counts as forensic flags for human review, never as automatic disqualification (UU PDP Pasal 10; PP 33 Pasal 120(2)(a)).
- Webcam snapshots or AI proctoring would add biometric data spesifik, a DPIA, cross-border transfer issues and a heavy consent burden. For a small team, a better path is controlled mode plus a supervised confirmation step (an onsite session or a live video call by the Psikolog).
- Audit trail fields:
  - session id, candidate id, form/bank version, scoring-key version, norm version
  - item served time, answer time and answer per item
  - disconnect and resume events, client user agent, IP (minimised)
  - proctor/Psikolog actions and score overrides with reason

### Gaps
- I found no Indonesian rule that specifically permits or restricts webcam proctoring or AI proctoring. Only the general PDP/biometric rules apply.
- No validated thresholds for response-time-based cheating detection were sourced. Item-level norms would have to be established empirically.

## 6. Fairness, accessibility, test-taker rights; documentation; "latihan" versus high-stakes labelling

### Takeaway
"Professional" means:
- documenting reliability, validity and norms for the Indonesian target population, including norming under internet/unproctored conditions
- monitoring DIF
- giving equivalent results across devices, or restricting devices
- WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility
- accommodations
- a feedback right (UU 23/2022 Pasal 44f)
- a complaints channel (Pasal 44e)
- a retake policy
- qualified-user gating of interpretation

A "latihan/simulasi" product can be legitimate if it uses non-operational items, makes no diagnostic or selection claims, and carries clear disclaimers. No Indonesian rule on labelling was found.

### Cited Findings
- UU 23/2022 Pasal 44(e)–(f): right to complain and get a response, and right to a "penjelasan yang benar dan jelas terkait hasil". Pasal 3(2)(d): nondiskriminasi. Pasal 33(2): "sahih dan andal" ([UU 23/2022](https://www.ipkindonesia.or.id/media/2022/08/Undang_Undang_No_23_Tahun_2022_tentang_Pendidikan_dan_Layanan_Psikologi.pdf)).
- PP 33 Pasal 31: processing must not be discriminatory. Pasal 6(1)(e): children's data is data spesifik, which matters for school-age test takers. UU PDP Pasal 25–26: parental consent for children; rules for data of persons with disabilities ([PP 33/2026](https://static.banyumaskab.go.id/website/file/peraturan_pemerintah_no._33_tahun_2026_100926115902.pdf); [pasal.id UU 27/2022](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-27-tahun-2022)).
- ITC CBT 1d/10: design for test-takers with disabilities and allow accommodations such as larger fonts. 2f/31: document fairness methods and assess DIF. 1a: account for "widely differing connection speeds" (guideline 1.8). 2c/22.3: norm under real internet conditions ([ITC 2005](https://www.intestcom.org/files/guideline_computer_based_testing.pdf)).
- ITC/ATP 2022 10.4: "A testing organization should require WCAG compliance … (currently WCAG 2.1 Level AA)". 10.5: allow breaks, extended time and other accommodations. 10.3: define constructs, so you can judge which accommodations preserve the construct. 7.4: comparability across devices, with measurement invariance and DIF as evidence. 3.27: the UI should adapt to device types ([ITC/ATP 2022](https://www.intestcom.org/upload/media-library/guidelines-for-technology-based-assessment-v20221108-16684036687NAG8.pdf)).
- ITC security: retest policies and waiting periods; test-taker rights documented and explained before the test ([ITC 2014](https://www.intestcom.org/files/guideline_test_security.pdf)).
- Kode Etik Pasal 65: interpretation must account for language and cultural differences (secondary, see section 2).
- Qualified-user gating: Pearson A/B/C levels ([Pearson](https://www.pearsonassessments.com/professional-assessments/ordering/how-to-order/qualifications.html)); BPS/EFPA three levels ([BPS](https://www.bps.org.uk/qualifications-and-fees)).
- Required documentation (ITC CBT 2b, 2d, 2e; ITC/ATP ch. 7):
  - minimum hardware and software requirements
  - supervision mode
  - scoring rules, including guessing penalties and unscored items
  - CBTI derivation and classification accuracy
  - equivalence evidence for paper-derived tests
  - norms and their conditions
  - report templates per audience, with stated limitations

### Inferences
- Minimum technical manual (synthesised from ITC 2005 2b–2e, ITC/ATP ch. 4 and 7, and UU 23/2022 Pasal 33(2)):
  - construct definitions
  - item development and review
  - reliability (internal consistency, plus SEM, or IRT information for CAT)
  - validity evidence for the selection use (criterion-related where possible)
  - norm sample description (N, region, education, age, gender, device, proctoring condition, year)
  - DIF analyses (gender, region/ethnolinguistic group, device)
  - CAT algorithm, stopping rule and exposure control
  - scoring and versioning
  - report interpretation limits
  - intended uses and user-qualification level
- Mobile versus desktop: either restrict operational speeded tests to desktop, or collect evidence of device comparability (ITC/ATP 7.4). For "latihan", allow any device but say that results are not comparable.
- Suggested "latihan/simulasi" disclaimer elements (inferred):
  - the results are not a psychological examination ("bukan pemeriksaan psikologis") and are not issued by a Psikolog
  - the items are not those of any official test
  - scores are indicative only, against a non-representative practice sample
  - not to be used for selection or diagnosis
  - data handling per the privacy notice
- Under UU 23/2022 Pasal 1(3), anything presented as an interpretation of a person's psychology "yang memerlukan kompetensi sebagai Psikolog" should be avoided in the latihan product.
- For an operational high-stakes tool, a small team realistically needs:
  - a partner Psikolog (STR + SILP) or a Biro Psikologi as the responsible professional
  - validated instruments, licensed or self-developed, with Indonesian norms
  - the PDP artefacts listed in section 3
  - supervised confirmation for decision-grade scores
  
  Without these, position the product as practice only.

### Gaps
- I found no Indonesian regulation or HIMPSI guidance on labelling of practice/simulation psikotes products, or on the legality of selling "latihan psikotes".
- I found no Indonesian accessibility law requirement specific to web tests. UU 8/2016 Penyandang Disabilitas and any Indonesian WCAG mandate for private platforms were not researched.
- I found no published Indonesian DIF or norming standards. HIMPSI test-adaptation requirements were not found.
- The AERA/APA/NCME Standards (2014) were not fetched. Their fairness and documentation chapters would strengthen the technical-manual checklist.
