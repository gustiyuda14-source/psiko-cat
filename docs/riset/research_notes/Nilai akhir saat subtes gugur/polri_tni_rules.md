# Polri/TNI rules: final psychology score when one sub-test is "Kurang Sekali" / TMS

Research date: 2026-10-16. Method: about 14 web searches and fetches. Primary PDFs downloaded and text-extracted: Pengumuman Kapolri Bintara TA 2026 and SIPSS TA 2025. Several tribratanews.*.polri.go.id pages could not be fetched (DNS failure), so quotes from them come from search-result snippets only and are marked **[snippet only]**.

## Q1. What do the Polri regulations and announcements say about nilai psikologi, the 61 threshold and gugur per aspek?

### Takeaway
The only official rule found is one sentence: a candidate is MS if the **nilai akhir** (final score) is **at least 61**. It cites Peraturan Asisten Kapolri Bidang SDM No. 3/2017. No official text found says that one aspect can make a candidate gugur. The same announcements do write out a per-item "nilai 0" knockout for the physical test (jasmani), and they write nothing like it for psychology.

### Cited Findings
- Bintara 2026 announcement (PENG/7/III/DIK.2.1./2026, dated 6 March 2026): "penilaian psikologi berdasarkan Peraturan Asisten Kapolri Bidang SDM nomor 3 tahun 2017 tentang Pelaksanaan Tes Psikologi Calon Anggota Kepolisian Negara Republik Indonesia dengan kategori memenuhi syarat (MS) apabila nilai akhir minimal 61;" — [Pengumuman Bintara Polri TA 2026 (PDF)](https://penerimaan.polri.go.id/uploads/pdf/PENGUMUMAN%20BINTARA%20POLRI%20TAHUN%20ANGGARAN%202026.pdf)
- Same document, test stages: "tes psikologi tahap I menggunakan sistem Computer Assisted Test (CAT) dengan penilaian secara kuantitatif dan kualitatif (MS/TMS)" and "tes psikologi tahap II (wawancara) dengan penilaian secara kualitatif (MS/TMS)". — [Bintara 2026 PDF](https://penerimaan.polri.go.id/uploads/pdf/PENGUMUMAN%20BINTARA%20POLRI%20TAHUN%20ANGGARAN%202026.pdf)
- Contrast in the same document, physical test: "kategori memenuhi syarat (MS) apabila Nilai Batas Lulus (NBL) 41,00 dengan masing-masing item tes tidak terdapat nilai "0" ... Bagi pendaftar semua Bintara Kompetensi Khusus (Bakomsus) tidak diberlakukan Nilai Batas Lulus (NBL) dan mengabaikan nilai "0"." This is a per-item knockout written out explicitly. Nothing equivalent appears for psychology. — [Bintara 2026 PDF](https://penerimaan.polri.go.id/uploads/pdf/PENGUMUMAN%20BINTARA%20POLRI%20TAHUN%20ANGGARAN%202026.pdf)
- SIPSS 2025 announcement (PENG/5/I/DIK.2.1./2025, dated 9 January 2025) has the same wording: "penilaian Tes Psikologi mempedomani Peraturan Asisten Kapolri Bidang Sumber Daya Manusia Nomor 3 tahun 2017 ... dengan kategori memenuhi syarat (MS) apabila nilai akhir minimal 61;" plus "pembobotan nilai hasil tes untuk menentukan perankingan peserta diatur dengan keputusan tersendiri." — [Pengumuman SIPSS TA 2025 (PDF)](https://penerimaan.polri.go.id/uploads/pdf/PENGUMUMAN_PENERIMAAN_SIPSS_T.A._2025.pdf)
- Search snippets show the same 61 / Perasisten SDM 3/2017 clause in the Tamtama Gelombang II 2024 and Tamtama Polair 2023 announcements. Direct downloads of those PDFs returned 404. — [Tamtama Gel. II 2024](https://penerimaan.polri.go.id/uploads/pdf/PENGUMUMAN%20TAMTAMA%20POLRI%20GELOMBANG%20II%20TAHUN%20ANGGARAN%202024.pdf); [Tamtama Polair 2023](https://penerimaan.polri.go.id/uploads/pdf/PENGUMUMAN_PENERIMAAN_TAMTAMA-POLAIR_TA_2023.pdf) **[snippet only]**
- Perkap 10/2016 on rekrutmen anggota Polri is hosted on Polda Kalsel. The search snippet only shows that psychology is one of the stages. Its text was not extracted. — [Perkap 10/2016 (PDF)](https://kalsel.polri.go.id/perkap/2016/PERKAP%20NOMOR%2010%20THN%202016%20TTG%20RIM%20ANGGOTA%20POLRI.pdf) **[not read]**
- Asisten SDM Polri Irjen Arief Sulistyanto, 2018: 32,814 PTU candidates (43.91%) were TMS, mostly for scores under the standard of 61. The weak points named per aspect were "berpikir logis (kecerdasan), pengambilan keputusan (kepribadian), dan ketahanan kerja (sikap kerja)". PTU uses a sistem gugur. Bakomsus is ranked on cumulative results instead. — [detikNews, 20 Apr 2018](https://news.detik.com/berita/d-3981156/32-814-peserta-seleksi-bintara-polri-gugur-di-tes-psikologi)

### Inferences
- In the official announcements, MS/TMS on the psychology test is defined only by the nilai akhir being 61 or more. Each announcement writes the jasmani "nilai 0" knockout out explicitly. A per-aspect psychology knockout, if one exists, is not public at announcement level.
- Perasisten SDM 3/2017 might contain a per-aspect rule, but its text was not found online.
- The 2018 statement describes weak aspects as the *reason* the total was low, not as separate knockouts. That fits a total-score rule.

### Gaps
- Full text of Peraturan Asisten Kapolri Bidang SDM No. 3/2017 was not found (not on jdih.polri.go.id search results). It is the key unknown.
- No Perpol after 2021 that specifically governs psychology scoring was found. The 2025 and 2026 announcements still cite the 2017 rule.
- The internal juknis of Bag Psi / Biro SDM was not found.

## Q2. How do published announcements lay out results? Do TMS candidates still show a numeric score?

### Takeaway
National announcements give the process and the threshold, not result tables. Regional (Polda) reports give MS/TMS counts and sometimes the highest and lowest scores. CAT shows candidates their score live. No published example was found of a candidate marked TMS whose shown score was 61 or above.

### Cited Findings
- On the CAT system, "Peserta tes dapat memonitor langsung nilai yang mereka dapatkan dalam sistem tersebut", and the results are "langsung diumumkan" (AKBP Zainal Arrahman, Kabag Dalpers Polda Sumsel). — [Liputan6, 19 May 2023](https://www.liputan6.com/bisnis/read/5291730/ketahui-apa-itu-sistem-cat-pada-tes-psikologi-penerimaan-polri)
- Polda NTT, Lekdiktuk BA 2025 CAT psychology: session 1 had 30 TMS, highest score 77, lowest 30; session 2 had 33 TMS. A numeric range is reported alongside the TMS count. — [Tribrata News NTT](https://tribratanews.ntt.polri.go.id/?p=2154) **[snippet only]**
- Polda Kepri, April 2025: 432 MS and 20 TMS reported as counts only. — [Tribrata News Kepri](https://tribratanews.kepri.polri.go.id/?p=12497) **[snippet only]**
- Polda Kaltim, 2017: 582 Bintara PTU and 57 Bintara TI MS; 366 PTU TMS. — [Tribrata News Kaltim 2017](https://tribratanews.kaltim.polri.go.id/2017/05/hasil-tes-psikologi-serentak-582-casis-bintara-ptu-dan-57-casis-bintara-ti-di-kaltim-dan-kaltara-dinyatakan-ms) **[snippet only]**

### Inferences
- The public format is a numeric nilai akhir plus MS/TMS, and MS/TMS follows from the 61 cutoff. This matches option (a) "shown as-is with status". The evidence found never needs a TMS status sitting next to a score of 61 or more.
- No evidence was found for option (b) capped, (c) zeroed or (d) score hidden.

### Gaps
- No per-candidate result PDF with columns (nama / nilai / keterangan) for 2021–2026 was obtained. Polda tribratanews pages were unreachable from this environment.
- SPCP 2026 result documents were not found.

## Q3. Is there a documented rule that "Kurang Sekali" in any aspect means TMS regardless of the total?

### Takeaway
No. The 0–40 / 41–60 / 61–80 / 81–100 category scale appears in Polda Kaltim news (2017–2018) for the **psychology score as a whole**. No source, official or bimbel, ties "Kurang Sekali" in a single aspect to automatic TMS.

### Cited Findings
- Polda Kaltim 2018 lists the categories "0 – 40 : Kurang sekali; 41 – 60 : Kurang; 61 – 80 : cukup; 81 – 100 : Baik" and states "nilai psikologi dinyatakan memenuhi syarat (MS) adalah 61 ke atas, dan tidak memenuhi syarat (TMS) adalah 60 ke bawah." The aspects named are kecerdasan, kepribadian and sikap kerja (kecepatan, ketelitian, ketahanan). — [Tribrata News Kaltim 2018](https://tribratanews.kaltim.polri.go.id/2018/04/penerimaan-polri-2018-di-kaltim-ribuan-casis-bintara-ikuti-tes-psikologi) **[snippet only; pre-2021]**
- A bimbel source says the final score is an accumulation of the aspects, so doing well in only one part is not enough. It does not mention a per-aspect knockout. — search summary of bimbel sites, e.g. [jadicasis.id](https://jadicasis.id/nilai-aman-psikologi-polri-seleksi/). The fetched jadicasis page itself only restates "nilai akhir minimal 61" and that weights are "diatur dengan keputusan tersendiri", with no per-aspect minimum.
- A Scribd document titled "Penilaian Tes Psikotes Polri" states a passing grade of 61. Its provenance is unknown. — [Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/860225882/PENILAIAN-TES-PSIKOTES-POLRI) **[snippet only, low credibility]**

### Inferences
- In the official-ish sources, "Kurang Sekali" is a band of the *overall* score. A Kurang Sekali total is TMS only because it is below 61, the same as "Kurang" (41–60).
- Treating "raw ≤40 in one aspect = Kurang Sekali = automatic TMS" is unsupported by public sources. If psiko-cat uses it, label it as a product or prep-course convention.

### Gaps
- Whether Perasisten SDM 3/2017 or the psychologists' internal assessment (the "kualitatif" part of "kuantitatif dan kualitatif (MS/TMS)") can give TMS on a profile basis is unknown. The phrase "kuantitatif dan kualitatif" leaves room for it, but no text confirms it.

## Q4. TNI (Akmil, Bintara, Tamtama): how are psychology results reported and how does gugur work?

### Takeaway
No public TNI source with psychology scoring rules, thresholds or per-aspect gugur mechanics was found. TNI announcements list psychology only as a selection stage.

### Cited Findings
- The Akmil/AAU/AAL requirements list stages (administrasi, kesehatan, jasmani, mental ideologi, psikologi, akademik) with no psychology threshold. — [Kompas TV 2021](https://www.kompas.tv/article/135627/tni-buka-pendaftaran-taruna-akmil-aau-dan-aal-tahun-2021-simak-informasinya-disini); [Antara](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/4419657/persyaratan-dan-cara-daftar-akmil-terbaru)
- Tamtama TNI AD 2021: testing covers administrasi, kesehatan jasmani, mental ideologi and psikologi, with no scoring detail. — [Kompas TV](https://www.kompas.tv/nasional/135556/tni-ad-juga-buka-rekrutmen-tamtama-dan-ini-cara-daftarnya?page=all)
- A bimbel site says only that for TNI the "sistem penilaian detailnya bisa berbeda" from Polri. — [jadiprajurit.id](https://jadiprajurit.id/nilai-psikologi-bintara-tertinggi/) (low credibility)

### Inferences
- TNI psychology results appear to be reported as MS/TMS or as a stage outcome, not as a publicly specified numeric formula. This is unverified.

### Gaps
- rekrutmen-tni.mil.id and Kodam/Rindam announcements were not reached. TNI psychology scoring rules (e.g. internal Dispsi juknis) are not public in what was searched.

## Q5. Are the 60/20/20 weights and "raw ≤40 = Kurang Sekali" official or prep-course conventions?

### Takeaway
They are prep-course conventions. The 60/20/20 split (kecerdasan / kepribadian / kecermatan) appears on a bimbel site, and a news article contradicts it with equal weighting. Neither is official. Official documents say only that weighting is "diatur dengan keputusan tersendiri". "≤40 = Kurang Sekali" comes from Polda Kaltim news (2017–2018) and applies to the overall score, not to a single aspect's raw score.

### Cited Findings
- 60/20/20 source (bimbel): "Kecerdasan (60%) ... 1 soal benar = 0,6 poin" for 100 questions in 90 minutes, max 60. "Kepribadian (20%)" is 100 Likert questions, max 20. "Kecermatan (20%)" is 10 columns × 50, "1 soal benar = 0,04 poin", max 20. The same site gives final Bintara weights of PTU akademik 35% / psikologi 35% / jasmani 30%. — [jadiprajurit.id](https://jadiprajurit.id/nilai-psikologi-bintara-tertinggi/) **(prep platform, low credibility, no official citation for the split)**
- Contradiction: Liputan6 (19 May 2023) says the three aspects have no separate percentage in the nilai akhir and carry equal weight. The fetched text quoted the sentence as "ada persentase pada masing-masing aspek", which is probably a dropped "tidak", since the surrounding meaning is equal weighting. This line is not attributed to a named official. — [Liputan6](https://www.liputan6.com/bisnis/read/5291730/ketahui-apa-itu-sistem-cat-pada-tes-psikologi-penerimaan-polri) (mainstream media, medium credibility)
- Official: "pembobotan nilai hasil tes untuk menentukan perankingan peserta diatur dengan keputusan tersendiri." — [SIPSS 2025 PDF](https://penerimaan.polri.go.id/uploads/pdf/PENGUMUMAN_PENERIMAAN_SIPSS_T.A._2025.pdf)

### Inferences
- The project's "Nilai Akhir Psikotest 60/20/20" is a defensible modelling convention but should not be presented as an official Polri formula.
- A raw score of 40 or below in one sub-test should not be presented as an official Kurang Sekali knockout either. The official public rule is a total of 61 or more.
- For psiko-cat display, the evidence best supports option (a): show the computed final score as-is plus an MS/TMS status. If the app also applies an internal per-aspect knockout, show it as a separate labelled flag (e.g. "aspek X Kurang Sekali — konvensi latihan"). Do not alter the number.

### Gaps
- The source of the specific 60/20/20 and 0.6/0.04-point scoring could not be traced. It may come from prep-book lore or a leaked juknis. Unverified.
