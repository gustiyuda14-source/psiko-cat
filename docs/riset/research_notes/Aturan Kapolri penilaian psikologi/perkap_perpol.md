# Perkap/Perpol hierarchy governing psychology testing in Polri selection

Research date: 2026-10-17. I extracted primary PDFs with pdftotext and quoted them verbatim. Anything taken only from search snippets or secondary sites is marked [UNVERIFIED].

Hierarchy in practice (2026):
UU 2/2002 → Perpres 52/2010 (last amended by Perpres 122/2024) → **Perkap 10/2016 (Penerimaan Calon Anggota Polri)**, which is still in force and cited in every 2026 announcement → Perkap 10/2016 Pasal 13(5) delegates the psychology stage to the "peraturan Kepala Satuan Fungsi" → **Peraturan Asisten Kapolri Bidang SDM No. 3 Tahun 2017 (Pelaksanaan Tes Psikologi Calon Anggota Polri)** → yearly Keputusan Kapolri (Kep/360, 361, 362/III/2026) and Pengumuman Kapolri (Peng/6, 7, 8/III/DIK.2.1./2026) → a separate Keputusan for pembobotan (weighting).

## Q1. Perkap 10/2016 and any Perpol on penerimaan (2021–2026)

### Takeaway
Perkap No. 10 Tahun 2016 is still the top-level recruitment regulation. I found no Perpol that replaces or amends it, and all 2025–2026 announcements still cite it. Its psychology content is thin: it defines "pemeriksaan psikologi", requires that selection results be announced "secara langsung, terbuka" and allows technology apps for this, and delegates the technical rules to the functional head, i.e. As SDM.

- **Delegation**, Pasal 13(5): "Kegiatan seleksi pada tahap sebagaimana dimaksud pada ayat (1) huruf a, huruf c sampai dengan huruf f dilaksanakan sesuai dengan peraturan Kepala Satuan Fungsi sesuai dengan kewenangannya masing-masing." Pasal 21(2) wewenang: "c. menentukan standar kelulusan", "g. melibatkan tim pemeriksa/outsourcing sesuai keahliannya" — [same](https://kalsel.polri.go.id/perkap/2016/PERKAP%20NOMOR%2010%20THN%202016%20TTG%20RIM%20ANGGOTA%20POLRI.pdf)
- Execution: Subpanitia Pusat (Pasal 22(1)e), Panitia Daerah (Pasal 23, "e. melaksanakan pemeriksaan psikologi") and Subpanitia Daerah (Pasal 24, "d. melaksanakan pemeriksaan psikologi") each "melaksanakan pemeriksaan psikologi". Each also has a complaint-handling duty and holds a "sidang terbuka" — [same](https://kalsel.polri.go.id/perkap/2016/PERKAP%20NOMOR%2010%20THN%202016%20TTG%20RIM%20ANGGOTA%20POLRI.pdf)
- Sidang kelulusan, Pasal 14: Panpus for Akpol and SIPSS; Panda for Bintara and Tamtama. Pasal 26: panitia "dapat meminta bantuan ahli dari instansi/lembaga lain" — [same](https://kalsel.polri.go.id/perkap/2016/PERKAP%20NOMOR%2010%20THN%202016%20TTG%20RIM%20ANGGOTA%20POLRI.pdf)
- Oversight, Pasal 27–31: internal oversight (pengawasan + pengamanan, i.e. Itwas + Propam) and external oversight. External oversight is "diatur dalam Peraturan Kapolri" (Pasal 29(2), i.e. Perkap 13/2010). Pasal 31: "Pengawas internal dan pengawas eksternal dilarang melakukan intervensi terhadap panitia". Pasal 32: evaluation by the "pengemban fungsi analisis dan evaluasi Staf Sumber Daya Manusia Polri" — [same](https://kalsel.polri.go.id/perkap/2016/PERKAP%20NOMOR%2010%20THN%202016%20TTG%20RIM%20ANGGOTA%20POLRI.pdf)

### Inferences
- Perkap 10/2016 has no psychology aspects, no instruments, no nilai akhir, no cut-off and no category scale. These all sit below it, by delegation under Pasal 13(5) and Pasal 21(1)c.
- There is **no formal sanggah/appeal procedure** for psychology results. The only remedies are complaints ("laporan atau komplain", Pasal 21–24) and post-failure counselling (tim konseling).
- Pasal 13(3) is the legal hook for live CAT score display ("diumumkan secara langsung, terbuka ... aplikasi teknologi").

### Gaps
- I found no Perpol (2019–2026) that amends or replaces Perkap 10/2016. I could not open jdih.polri.go.id directly, so a 2026 amendment cannot be fully ruled out. The fact that all March 2026 announcements still cite Perkap 10/2016 strongly suggests none exists.
- I did not see Articles 4–9 and 16–20 of Perkap 10/2016 in detail (persyaratan, jenis penerimaan, panitia structure). They do not contain "psikolog" except where quoted above.

## Q2. Perkap/Perpol on psychological services; role of Biro Psikologi, Bag Psi and psikolog

### Takeaway
I found no general "Perkap/Perpol tentang Psikologi Kepolisian" that defines recruitment testing. The closest Perkap-level psychology-test regulation is **Perkap 10/2021**, and it covers firearms candidates only, not recruitment. It is a useful template: it defines psikolog and tester, aspects, MS/TMS, psikogram and re-test. Organizationally, psychology sits in Biro Psikologi SSDM Polri at Mabes and in the psychology section of Biro SDM at Polda level.

### Inferences
- If the recruitment rule (Perasisten SDM 3/2017) mirrors Perkap 10/2021, it likely also sets psikolog-signed results and a psikogram. That is only an analogy, and Perkap 10/2021 is not a recruitment rule.
- Naming inconsistency: the 2026 announcements call the 2025 amendments "Peraturan Kepala Kepolisian Nomor 4/6 Tahun 2025" while describing them as amending "Peraturan Kepolisian Negara RI" (Perpol). Since 2018 the instrument has been "Peraturan Kepolisian" (Perpol), so the "Kepala" label is probably a drafting error in the announcement.

### Gaps
- I could not access the text of Perpol 6/2017 as amended by 4/2025, so I have no exact tugas clause for Biro Psikologi SSDM.
- I did not confirm whether a Perpol on "Psikologi Kepolisian" exists. None turned up in any search.

## Q3. Does any Perkap/Perpol define "nilai akhir psikologi", aspects, or per-aspect disqualification?

### Takeaway
No. Neither Perkap 10/2016 nor any Perpol I found defines nilai akhir, aspects or per-aspect gugur. These are delegated to **Peraturan Asisten Kapolri Bidang SDM No. 3 Tahun 2017**, and the announcements only repeat its MS threshold of "nilai akhir minimal 61". Weighting for ranking goes into a separate Keputusan ("diatur dengan keputusan tersendiri").

### Cited Findings
- Perkap 10/2016 Pasal 13(5) delegates psychology to the "peraturan Kepala Satuan Fungsi" — [Perkap 10/2016](https://kalsel.polri.go.id/perkap/2016/PERKAP%20NOMOR%2010%20THN%202016%20TTG%20RIM%20ANGGOTA%20POLRI.pdf)
- Every 2026 announcement says: "penilaian psikologi berdasarkan Peraturan Asisten Kapolri Bidang SDM nomor 3 tahun 2017 tentang Pelaksanaan Tes Psikologi Calon Anggota Kepolisian Negara Republik Indonesia dengan kategori memenuhi syarat (MS) apabila nilai akhir minimal 61" — [Bintara 2026](https://penerimaan.polri.go.id/uploads/pdf/PENGUMUMAN%20BINTARA%20POLRI%20TAHUN%20ANGGARAN%202026.pdf); [Akpol 2026](https://penerimaan.polri.go.id/uploads/pdf/PENGUMUMAN%20TARUNA%20AKPOL%20T.A.%202026.pdf); [Tamtama 2026](https://penerimaan.polri.go.id/uploads/pdf/PENGUMUMAN%20TAMTAMA%20POLRI%20TAHUN%20ANGGARAN%202026.pdf) ("mempedomani"); [SIPSS 2025](https://penerimaan.polri.go.id/uploads/pdf/PENGUMUMAN_PENERIMAAN_SIPSS_T.A._2025.pdf)
- Bintara 2026, 5.u: "Pembobotan nilai hasil tes untuk menentukan kelulusan dan peringkat peserta, diatur dengan keputusan tersendiri". Akpol 2026, angka 6: the weighting is "diatur dengan keputusan tersendiri" — [Bintara 2026](https://penerimaan.polri.go.id/uploads/pdf/PENGUMUMAN%20BINTARA%20POLRI%20TAHUN%20ANGGARAN%202026.pdf); [Akpol 2026](https://penerimaan.polri.go.id/uploads/pdf/PENGUMUMAN%20TARUNA%20AKPOL%20T.A.%202026.pdf)
- Contrast with physical tests: the announcements do state a per-item rule for jasmani, "NBL 41,00 dengan masing-masing item tes tidak terdapat nilai '0'". No equivalent per-aspect rule is stated for psychology — [Bintara 2026](https://penerimaan.polri.go.id/uploads/pdf/PENGUMUMAN%20BINTARA%20POLRI%20TAHUN%20ANGGARAN%202026.pdf)
- Secondary claims about the content of Perasisten 3/2017 [UNVERIFIED, commercial bimbel or news]: the three aspects are kecerdasan, kepribadian and kecermatan/sikap kerja; passing requires at least "C (Memenuhi Syarat)" on the nilai akhir and "TIDAK ADA KS di aspek manapun" — [latihancat.com](https://www.latihancat.com/psikologi-polri/). Liputan6 says the aspects have equal weight with no fixed percentage — [Liputan6](https://www.liputan6.com/bisnis/read/5291730/ketahui-apa-itu-sistem-cat-pada-tes-psikologi-penerimaan-polri)
- Example result spread: in the Polda Sumsel Akpol CAT psychology test (2025), 204 candidates were MS and 28 TMS, with a highest score of 77 and lowest of 32 [news] — [liputanabn](https://liputanabn.com/232-catar-akpol-hari-ini-jalani-seleksi-cat-psikologi-tingkat-panda/)

### Inferences
- The only official, citable numeric rule is "nilai akhir minimal 61 = MS". The per-aspect KS gugur rule and the aspect list rest only on non-official sources until the Perasisten 3/2017 text is obtained.

### Gaps
- The full text of Perasisten SDM 3/2017 is not public online (not on jdih or penerimaan.polri.go.id). Its definitions of aspects, scale and per-aspect gugur therefore remain unverified.
- The 2026 Keputusan on pembobotan is not public.

## Q4. 2025–2026 Pengumuman Kapolri: psychology wording and legal bases

### Takeaway
The Bintara, Tamtama and Akpol 2026 announcements (all dated 6 March 2026, signed a.n. Kapolri by As SDM u.b. Karodalpers, Brigjen Erthel Stephan) share the same 10 rujukan. Psychology runs in two stages: Tahap I by CAT, scored "kuantitatif dan kualitatif (MS/TMS)", and Tahap II as an interview scored "kualitatif (MS/TMS)". HIMPSI is named as an outsourcing expert, and external overseers may not decide kelulusan.

### Inferences
- "Kuantitatif dan kualitatif (MS/TMS)" for the CAT stage means a numeric score (the nilai akhir, which feeds weighting and ranking) plus an MS/TMS gate at 61. The interview stage is purely qualitative MS/TMS.
- Appeal routes are complaint, hotline and WBS for misconduct only. There is no formal re-scoring or sanggah for psychology results.

## Q5. SPCP 2026 or new CAT psychology rules

### Takeaway
I found no new regulation (Perpol, Perasisten or Kep) for 2021–2026 that replaces Perasisten SDM 3/2017 or creates new CAT psychology rules. Every 2025–2026 announcement still cites the 2017 rule. CAT is a delivery mode set in the announcements, not in any Perkap.

### Cited Findings
- Perasisten SDM 3/2017 is cited unchanged in SIPSS 2025 and in Bintara, Tamtama and Akpol 2026 — [SIPSS 2025](https://penerimaan.polri.go.id/uploads/pdf/PENGUMUMAN_PENERIMAAN_SIPSS_T.A._2025.pdf); [Bintara 2026](https://penerimaan.polri.go.id/uploads/pdf/PENGUMUMAN%20BINTARA%20POLRI%20TAHUN%20ANGGARAN%202026.pdf)
- The CAT requirement appears only in the announcements ("menggunakan sistem Computer Assisted Test (CAT)"). Perkap 10/2016 only allows "sarana aplikasi teknologi" for announcing results (Pasal 13(3)) — [Bintara 2026](https://penerimaan.polri.go.id/uploads/pdf/PENGUMUMAN%20BINTARA%20POLRI%20TAHUN%20ANGGARAN%202026.pdf); [Perkap 10/2016](https://kalsel.polri.go.id/perkap/2016/PERKAP%20NOMOR%2010%20THN%202016%20TTG%20RIM%20ANGGOTA%20POLRI.pdf)
- In 2026 practice, Polda-run Tahap II psychology interviews are held individually by the "tim pemeriksa psikologi Polri" [news] — [RRI Jayapura](https://rri.co.id/jayapura/regional/2496787/ratusan-peserta-seleksi-calon-bintara-polri-mengikuti-pemeriksaan-psikologi); [Humas Polri, Polda Sumsel](https://humas.polri.go.id/news/detail/2426689-polda-sumsel-perkuat-sdm-unggul-melalui-seleksi-ketat-wawancara-psikologi-bintara-polri-2026)

### Inferences
- Any "live score" display for CAT psychology rests on the Perkap 10/2016 Pasal 13(3) transparency clause plus Panpus discretion (Pasal 21(1)c). It is not mandated by any specific psychology regulation.
